Real-World Giants

See how the principles you just simulated are used by the world's largest companies.

📦 Amazon

0+

Robots in fulfillment centers worldwide.

  • Network: 200+ US fulfillment centers, 80,000 trailers, 100 cargo aircraft.
  • Automation: Robots like Hercules transport inventory pods to workers.
  • Speed: Advanced forecasting allows two-to-five day delivery speeds.
  • Scale: Handles over 1 million parcels per day at a single automated facility.

📱 Apple

$0 Billion

Revenue in fiscal 2025, mostly from design and software.

  • Design: Designs in California but owns almost no factories.
  • Assembly: China assembles iPhone Pro models; India now assembles ~25% of all iPhones.
  • Components: Processors from TSMC in Taiwan, memory from South Korea and Japan.
  • Materials: Investing $500 million in US rare-earth magnet production.

Glossary of Global Terms

Hover (or tap) a card to reveal its definition!

Supply Chain

The steps a product goes through from raw materials to your hands.

Redundancy

Having more than one path or backup so the system can recover from problems.

Specialization

When different places focus on the work they do best.

Global Interconnection

Different parts of the world depending on one another.

Resilience

The ability of a network to survive shocks and keep working.

Interdependence

When countries or people rely on each other to function.

Logistics

The process of planning and moving goods around the world.

Identity

The qualities, beliefs, and experiences that make you who you are.
